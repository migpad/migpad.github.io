MigPad

A small, fast text editor for macOS, Linux and Windows, in the spirit of AkelPad.

Download

macOS

MigPad.dmg

macOS 10.15.7 or later, Apple silicon and Intel. Drag MigPad to Applications.

Windows

x64InstallerZIP
ARM64InstallerZIP

Windows 10 version 1903 or later and Windows 11. The ZIP is portable: MigPad keeps its data next to it. MigPad is not signed yet: if SmartScreen warns about it, choose More info, then Run anyway.

Linux

x64.deb.rpmAppImage
ARM64.deb.rpmAppImage

Ubuntu 22.04, Debian 12, Fedora 36 and later, with a Vulkan driver. The AppImage runs without installing, once made executable.

MigPad never goes online, so it does not look for updates: new versions are here and on GitHub, with what changed.

What it is

  • Instant It starts at once, uses little memory and keeps large files responsive.
  • Careful with text Unicode and legacy encodings — Windows-1251, KOI8-R, CP866 and more — their detection, BOM, LF, CRLF and CR line endings; saving never loses a character unnoticed.
  • Nothing is lost Edits are saved as you type: quitting asks nothing and brings back the windows, tabs and unsaved changes, even after a crash.
  • Find and replace With regular expressions, whole words and case; go to a line and column.
  • Offline No telemetry, accounts or online services.
  • Everything built in No plugins to collect: tabs and windows, a command line, settings in a text file, English and Russian.
